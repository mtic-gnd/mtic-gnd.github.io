Frequently asked questions

Is the quote free?

Yes. We check the computer, explain what is wrong and give you a price before doing anything, with no obligation.

Do you repair any brand?

Yes: HP, Lenovo, Asus, Acer, Dell, MSI, Apple and custom-built PCs, both laptops and desktops.

Will I lose my photos and documents?

We always try to keep your data. Before any repair that could affect it, we offer to make a backup.

My computer is very slow. Is it worth fixing?

Often, yes: replacing the hard drive with an SSD, adding RAM and cleaning it inside can make it feel new for much less than buying another one. If it isn't worth it, we will tell you.
